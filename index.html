About

I am a PhD candidate at the Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing (UKP) Lab at Technical University of Darmstadt, supervised by Prof. Dr. Iryna Gurevych. My research focuses on gaining a deeper understanding of language models to unlock their full potential as valuable societal tools.

During my PhD, I have examined knowledge integration, out-of-distribution generalization, linguistic competence, and the interplay between model internals and behavior for toxicity.

Research Interests

  • Understanding model internals and their interplay with model behavior
  • Unleashing the power of latent abilities encoded in model internals
  • Tracing generalization capabilities of models for future scenarios
  • Computational argumentation and stance detection
